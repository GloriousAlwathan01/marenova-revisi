Our Company

Marenova Surveyors is an independent inspection and survey company serving the shipping, commodity, energy and insurance sectors.

What business we are in

We provide the independent facts behind every cargo and vessel transaction. Whether a bulk shipment is loaded, a vessel changes hands or a tow crosses open water, our surveyors verify what is on board, in what condition, and in what quantity, then document it in a report that holds up with counterparties.

Our work covers five service lines: inspection, survey, sampling and analysis, certification and verification, and consultancy. Each is delivered by trained field staff following written procedures, applicable standards and the scope agreed with the client.

Because we have no commercial interest in the cargo or the vessel, our findings stay objective, which is exactly what buyers, sellers, owners, charterers and underwriters need when a claim or a dispute arises.

Vision

To become a leading maritime surveying company at both national and international levels, recognized for its integrity, precision, and service excellence, in supporting the safety and efficiency of the maritime, energy, and global trade industries.

Mission

  • To deliver accurate, independent, and reliable marine and inspection services in accordance with international standards.
  • To build long-term partnerships with clients through integrity, professionalism, and transparency.
  • To develop highly skilled and experienced professionals in the fields of marine, petroleum & gas surveying, and related sectors.
  • To prioritize safety, efficiency, and environmental sustainability in every aspect of operations.
  • To continuously innovate by utilizing modern technology and information systems to enhance service quality and operational performance.

Key Enablers

  • Competent people. Qualified surveyors with field experience in bulk, liquid and project cargo.
  • Written procedures. Standard methods for sampling, measurement and reporting.
  • Laboratory network. Access to accredited labs for chemical and physical testing.
  • Digital reporting. Photo-backed reports with a clear, auditable trail.

Competitive Advantages

  • Independence. No stake in the cargo, vessel or outcome.
  • Responsiveness. Local teams near the main loading and discharge ports.
  • Full scope. One partner from pre-shipment inspection to post-discharge verification.
  • Clear reporting. Findings written for commercial and technical readers alike.
